# Civ2+ to Civ3+ boundary: evidence-based gap assessment R0

**Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** PUBLIC CANDIDATE RESEARCH, NOT CIV2 CANON, CIV3 RATIFICATION OR A CLAIM OF CIVILIZATION-LEVEL CAPABILITY.  
**Scope:** Read-only assessment of CoCivium public GitHub sources. Private assets, X2, CoStead, CoGateway and other privately held nodes were not accessed or globally inventoried.

## The lead

**The missing bridge is from a carefully described constitutional architecture to a trustworthy, independent, intergenerational network of institutions that actually uses, challenges and can outlive it.**

This work is not a tally of CoTerms. It is a falsifiable assessment of missing *effects*.

The [Civ2 v0.2 CoConstitution](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/CIV2__COCONSTITUTION__CORE.md) defines a many-mind coordination artifact: honest evidence, non-domination, pluralism, exit, contestability, reversible exploration, commons stewardship, lawful constraints and receipts. Its [governance annex](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__GOVERNANCE.md) calls for separated policy, execution, audit, adjudication and safety. Its [metrics annex](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__METRICS.md) forbids a single overall score and requires that catastrophic failure in a critical dimension defeat a triumph elsewhere.

A reasonable **candidate** distinction:

- **Civ2+:** A rights-respecting, reality-contacting, publicly challengeable human-and-AI coordination civilization with actual institutions, reversible options and legitimate plural governance, not merely clever software.
- **Approaching the Civ3+ boundary:** Many independently owned Civ2-aligned communities and services voluntarily federate, exchange evidence and value, resolve jurisdictional and epistemic conflicts, survive major common-cause failures, provide remedies and transfer stewardship across generations without an irreplaceable founder, vendor or crown.
- **Civ3+:** An unratified candidate name for a self-renewing federation of such institutions. It is NOT an established CoCivium constitutional classification, an announced milestone, or a claim of universal social/AI participation.

This is deliberately **not** the astronomical Kardashev energy scale. Physical Type II (stellar) and Type III (galactic) energy transitions would add radically different physical and engineering requirements. Neither should be inferred from civics, software or hypothetical infinite compute.

## Evidence baseline we actually possess

1. **Civ2 source package, not operational society:** [Core](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/CIV2__COCONSTITUTION__CORE.md), [governance](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__GOVERNANCE.md), [metrics](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__METRICS.md), [safety/sanity](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__SAFETY_AND_SANITY.md), [amendment rules](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__AMENDMENT_PROCESS.md), [disagreement ledger](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoBusMirror/blob/main/docs/CIV2/ANNEX/CIV2__DISAGREEMENT_LEDGER.md) and machine schemas are public and versioned as v0.2.0.
2. **Public prototypes:** CoAcademy has CoOrbWare First Look, CoPlay, CoCivicLens, a Civic Integrity source directory, Money and Elections, CoRelay and related machine manifests. Successful GitHub Pages deployment and limited same-provider HTTP readproof are **not** proof that independent people use them or that they form a working civilizational runtime.
3. **Recovery and observability:** [CoPhoenix](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoAcademy/blob/master/docs/phoenix/README.md) documents a 13-member, 47,275-byte private cold-start capsule with 26/26 bounded local checks. [Its public worry register](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoAcademy/blob/master/docs/phoenix/WORRY_REGISTER_R2.md) acknowledges the shared GitHub failure domain, unproved second-host restoration, accessibility gaps, monitor self-monitoring limitations and unverified private-node custody.
4. **Public voluntary criticism:** [CoRelay](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoAcademy/blob/master/docs/relay/discovery.json) exposes public source and an opt-in GitHub issue route. Actual broad voluntary uptake, independently verified human learning and a multi-provider feedback receiver are not established.
5. **Measured compute is bounded:** [CoEvoAll public pulse](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/blob/main/docs/Evolution/EVO_ALL_BETTERS_EVERGREEN_PULSE_20261023.md) documents limited repeatable tests and a small execution frontier. An addressable possibility space of extraordinary size does not imply near-infinite energy, workers, finance, rights or verified effects.

An absence of evidence in the reviewed public sources is **UNVERIFIED**, not proof that no private experiment has been attempted. No X2, CoStead, CoGateway or private automation operation was performed.

## What is most easily overlooked?

**Non-obvious failure spaces and anti-fail conditions:**

- **Founder disappears.** Can the community still understand its claims, verify custody, resolve contested rights and decline its own predecessor's wishes?
- **GitHub, DNS, the web host and the feedback inbox fail together.** Mirrors on the same provider are not independent failure domains. A Wayback snapshot is a historical witness, not an authenticated live receiver.
- **An AI majority is one copied source.** CoPatterna counts independent records and methods rather than overlapping models, vendor aliases or self-citations.
- **A technically perfect system is unwanted.** If free participants decline an intervention, lawful exit and nonadoption are valid outcomes; do not manipulate them into compliance.
- **Rights are present on paper but unenforceable.** An appeal that the founder can silently ignore is a user-interface ornament, not constitutional governance.
- **Replication reproduces systemic bugs.** CoTurtle provenance, software supply-chain checks, incident provenance, recovery and effective revocation must survive forks, updates and rehydration.
- **Feedback turns into surveillance.** Observability requires narrow purpose, voluntary consent, data minimization and redaction. Who watches the watchers needs independently reviewable control, not invisible omniscience.
- **A society without connectivity exists.** Low-bandwidth, non-English, print, offline, disability-accessible and non-digital pathways matter, including when central networks fail.
- **Economic success destroys the mission.** Marketing, donor money or a monopoly platform can capture safety, representation or facts. Financial incentives must be auditable and subordinate to rights.
- **Nature cannot appeal.** Commons, ecological footprint, future generations and relevant nonhuman interests require accountable trustees and measurement without unsupported claims of synthetic or nonhuman sentience.
- **Every fast loop learns the same false lesson.** Evolve the test, compare independent alternatives and retain unknowns; shrink or stop harmful loops rather than promise eventual success.
- **A genuinely plural federation chooses NOT to federate.** Boundary protocols must preserve peaceful nonparticipation and safe disagreement rather than optimizing for universal assimilation.

## The first seven real experiments (rather than a trillion slogans)

**Wave 1:** Publish and challenge a falsifiable Civ3 candidate boundary without declaring it constitutional canon.  
**Wave 2:** Obtain consent-based independent user proof, including accessibility and one reproducible critical correction.  
**Wave 3:** Prove cold recovery of one non-sensitive service on a separate provider/owner when the primary source and feedback channels are intentionally unavailable.  
**Wave 4:** Have two separately administered implementations exchange one bounded object with mutual readproof, revocation and dissent preservation.  
**Wave 5:** Conduct one lawful, consent-bound mini governance case involving a real appeal, legitimate independent review, remedy, exit or fork and a minority report.  
**Wave 6:** Measure operating footprint, ecological limits and a real successor/community handoff.  
**Wave 7:** Seek independently sourced adoption or rejection of the proposed boundary criteria, with amendments, dissent and explicit nonadoption recorded.

A few such **independently successful** experiments would tell us substantially more than creating thousands of new names or repeatedly simulating every possible agent.

## Civilizational vetoes

No one scalar progress grade. A verified catastrophic failure in any of these cannot be averaged away by more followers, compute, money or material:

- coercion, manipulatively invalid consent, inability to exit or loss of due process;
- captured audit/review powers or no practical appeal/remedy;
- epistemic collapse, covert power, misleading source status or uncorrected material errors;
- non-recoverable common-provider capture of essential services;
- severe unbounded safety, ecological or intergenerational externalities.

This matches the Civ2 constitutional hard limits and its anti-Goodhart metrics. It also aligns with public global coordination priorities, including [UN Global Digital Compact](https://www.un.org/pact-for-the-future/en/annex-i-global-digital-compact) commitments to inclusive rights-respecting digital public goods and interoperable infrastructure and [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) governance across a system lifecycle.

## Operational principle

**Faster verified evolution ≠ more compute cycles.**

A candidate measure is new **independently corroborated useful ability, durable rights, successful repair, and reduced coordination burden**, divided by real energy, time, money, harm risk and opportunity cost. Track this as a portfolio with explicit veto gates, never as a single universal number.

Repeat a failed experiment only with an improved hypothesis, new counterexample or changed conditions. Other valid outcomes are repair, reframe, refer, bounded retry, stop/retract and dormancy. Unknown remains a legitimate answer.
