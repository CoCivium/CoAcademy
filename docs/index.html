From a world-scale problem to one verifiable change

Ambition: Make trustworthy knowledge useful to everyone, not just people with time, money, and specialist training.

  1. Causes: Inaccessible explanations and untraceable claims leave people unable to challenge decisions.
  2. Leverage: Link every claim to readable evidence, alternatives and accessible presentations.
  3. First test: Can a newcomer explain and contest one complex claim after a short, source-linked encounter?
  4. What would change our minds? If readers remain confused or cannot find evidence, redesign the explanation and measure again.
