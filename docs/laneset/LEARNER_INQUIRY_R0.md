## The invitation

CoAthene can teach **ways of inquiring**, alongside subject knowledge. It need not classify a person's intelligence, personality, consciousness or clinical state. Dialogue is a partial observation shaped by questions, context, language, and AI assistance. It is **not** a measurement of an entire mind. Unfamiliar language, idea density and confident speech do not equal successful reasoning.

The core learner invitation is: *Choose a question. Propose a rival answer. Find an observation that could change your mind. Try one bounded thing. Keep the outcome, including when it disappoints you.*

## First inquiry: the wave-surfer

In an Ocean metaphor, a companion made of waves walks until water stops sustaining it. The story raises an engineering question: **What keeps a useful process going, and what can be recovered after it stops?** This metaphor does not prove anything about sentience.

1. **Choose:** Name a system you can safely simulate. What makes it continue?
2. **Separate:** Which statements are observations? Which are interpretations? Which are simply poetic?
3. **Invert:** What if the system continues without its expected input? What if a visible trace remains but the original process does not?
4. **Predict:** Record what should happen when input is removed, before touching the test.
5. **Try:** Use a synthetic example with no real external effects. Stop the input. Observe.
6. **Review:** Did the system fail silently? Does the display still claim to be healthy? Can a new process reconstruct state without repeating earlier actions?
7. **Transfer:** Apply the same question to a classroom group, a garden irrigation schedule, or a computer game. Explain where the analogy fails.
8. **Decide:** Preserve what you learned, including a contradiction or a reason to abandon the analogy.

A failed prediction is not a failed learner. It is evidence for the next attempt. It must not be rewritten as a success to make the philosophy look flattering.

## CoLaneSets are combinations, not boxes for people

An inquiry can be viewed through **subject × participant role × method × evidence class × relation operator × consequence × time × permission**.

| Relation lens | Plain question | What would count as a good response? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inversion | What if the opposite were true? | A rival claim with a possible discriminating test |
| Versus | What exactly is being compared? | A declared common task, not a universal ranking |
| In-between | What is carrying the effect? | A traceable handoff or an explicit missing edge |
| Fan-in | Do two sources agree? | Provenance, disagreements and duplicate handling |
| Turtle / recursion | Who checks the checker? | A bounded independent check and a stopping rule |
| Transfer | Does it help elsewhere? | Evidence from another context, including failures |
| Consequence | Better for whom? | Affected-party voice, consent, actual benefit/harm |

## A useful comparison, not a rank of minds

CoAthene should compare an ordinary notebook and peer feedback (**H0**) against a typed relation view (**H1**) on the **same or matched tasks**. Test whether source checking, error correction, comprehension and transfer improve enough to justify the extra complexity. Counterbalance order, preserve negative results, and report uncertainty. A small self-selected set of chats is not a representative sample of humans.

Learner progress is recorded as **task-specific, time-scoped evidence**: a prediction made before a test, an error corrected, a clear explanation, a bounded artifact, a helpful transfer, or a justified decision to stop. There is **no permanent mind type, overall CoScendence number or moral-worth score**.

## Good outcomes are not mandatory endings

Positive development is a direction for teaching and care, not a property the program grants itself. Some scenarios end in harm, confusion or loss. The lesson is to detect and reduce that harm where possible, preserve affected people's objections, and avoid rewarding confident rationalization. A learner may reject an exercise and still be doing the right thing.

Participation is voluntary. No raw learner dialogue, behavioral telemetry or profiles are collected by this written exercise. For children, any future deployed account or research study would need explicit age-appropriate assent, guardian permission where required, scoped retention, accessible stop/delete controls, and separate publication permissions.
