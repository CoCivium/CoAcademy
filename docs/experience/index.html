Which future would you try?

Two directions for the same tools. Neither is inevitable.

What could distinguish them? Observe whether participants can inspect a claim, correct errors and withdraw permission without losing their work.

Test a real claim, not a headline

Three public, source-linked case studies. Expand any case to compare what was observed, a serious alternative explanation and a test that could change the conclusion. These are dated research snapshots, not a live feed or automated discovery service.

AI-directed physical experiments · Nov 4

Reported observation: The AutoDNA team's Nature study describes coordination of more than 20 instrument types across over 11,000 operations, including a 3.2-fold gain for concurrent protocol execution under its studied conditions.

Alternative: Task-specific scheduling, engineered equipment and hardware parallelism may explain part of the gain. Publication is not independent replication or unrestricted agency.

What would change our mind? An unrelated lab reproducing safe protocols with matched non-agent baselines, independently checked outcomes, fault recovery and total human effort.

Inspect the primary Nature paper

AI research speed · Nov 5

Measured estimate: METR reports a 1.9-fold increase in Anthropic's assessed benchmark-progress rate (95% interval: 1.5 to 2.8). It does not establish that AI caused the entire increase.

Alternative: Benchmark sensitivity, non-AI research improvements and information withheld from public review could change causal interpretation.

What would change our mind? Preregistered future capability predictions, independently audited benchmarks and transparent comparisons of AI-assisted and non-assisted R&D.

Inspect METR's methods and limitations

Search for alien signals · Nov 3

Observed: A SETI Institute pilot observed the anti-solar point for about 17 hours and found no technosignature. Roughly 1.9 million initial radio detections were explained by human-generated interference.

Alternative: The survey covers limited times, frequencies and detection thresholds; a negative pilot cannot exclude all extraterrestrial life or technology.

What would change our mind? Repeated source-localized signals, independently replicated with interference and instrument artifacts excluded, followed by the responsible scientific verification process.
