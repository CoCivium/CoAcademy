PUBLIC RESEARCH · NOT A FINISHED PRODUCT

CoCivium is exploring how people, AI tools, devices and tasks can coordinate around human-selected goals. The practical problem is the burden of connecting everything manually.

WHAT WE CAN VERIFY

CoCivium has a publicly accessible repository of experimental research and working source files.

WHAT WE ARE NOT CLAIMING

A public repository does not prove a universally working cross-device AI or operating system.

CONOT+ · WHAT REMAINS TO BE TESTED

Independent new-user usability, accessibility and verified outcome measurements.
