If I Had a Penny

Small-contribution concepts

Consider how modest contributions of time, ideas or resources might create shared value. No payment or donation service is offered.

This is an exploratory page, not an operational service. Its purpose and scope may evolve.

Try a small exercise

  1. Choose a non-monetary contribution.
  2. Define who benefits.
  3. Describe how to verify the effect.
