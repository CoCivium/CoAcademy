## Lead: what can a visitor actually do?

**Find authoritative election information, understand why election-night numbers are unofficial, learn what audits do, and reach responsible authorities for serious concerns.**

This is a low-risk public education prototype, not an election-security service, campaign, complaint mailbox, government site, product certification, or statement that any particular election was corrupt.

Current interactions: three synthetic source-literacy examples. The page is static and does not accept names, accusations, addresses, ballot records, political affiliations or private reports.

## Why the work is plausible

- Election administration is carried out primarily by states and localities in the U.S. <https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/election-results-and-voting-information/>.
- Election-night results are unofficial. Canvassing, post-election checks and certification follow procedures that vary by jurisdiction. <https://www.eac.gov/election-officials/election-results-canvass-and-certification>.
- Election audits exist, but the methods and requirements vary. <https://electionlab.mit.edu/research/post-election-audits>.
- Suspected voter intimidation, suppression, or fraud have existing official reporting channels. <https://www.usa.gov/voter-fraud>.
- International IDEA's 2026 report and Freedom House's 2026 report document broad challenges to democracy, based on different methodologies. Those studies are *not* evidence of fraud in a named election. <https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/global-state-democracy-2026-democracy-age-conflict> and <https://freedomhouse.org/report/freedom-world/2026/growing-shadow-autocracy>.

## Six lanes for independent review

| Reviewer role | One useful challenge | What would qualify as evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Election administrator | Does the page accurately distinguish unofficial reporting, canvass, audit and certification for a stated jurisdiction? | Public legal/procedural source, current dated citation, exact correction |
| Election-law or civil-rights specialist | Are official reporting channels framed responsibly and without obstructing urgent support? | Review of applicable route, rights, privacy and remedy |
| Accessibility practitioner | Can a keyboard-only, mobile or screen-reader visitor reach official resources without depending on the illustration? | Actual device/browser readproof and reproducible issue report |
| Security/privacy reviewer | Is there any hidden collection, personal-data exposure, unsafe link or unintended allegation publication? | Code review, independent network check, threat-model counterexample |
| Researcher/journalist | Does the page separate primary documentation, interpretation, unknowns and unverified allegations? | Traceable original sources and visible nonclaims |
| Human/AI usability evaluator | Can a first-time reader independently find one official answer and explain one process without false certainty? | Consent-based observations or a source-linked model audit, with errors logged |

No named person, organization, model, university, board member or election office is represented as a collaborator, sponsor, reviewer or endorser unless separately confirmed.

## The real problem to solve

Public election communication is fragmented across many jurisdictions, procedural steps and institutions. CoCivium could help with source routing, accessible explanations, evidence provenance, translation integrity and verification literacy. **It must not presume authority to audit real ballots, run election infrastructure, adjudicate claims, collect sensitive reports or influence people's political choices.**

Three distinctions must survive every derivative:

1. **Public allegation != finding.** Never amplify unsupported accusations, private citizen data or personally identifying records.
2. **Election-night total != certified result.** Normal updating is not, on its own, evidence of wrongdoing.
3. **Source or simulation != approved election tool.** Election-system access, official partnerships, risk-limiting audit calculations and outside-user outcomes are not established.

## Candidate product roadmap

**Before November 3, 2026, if feasible:**
- Independent HTTPS and mobile/browser check of the public page.
- Neutral review of every official route and claim.
- Accessibility review with a disability-community reviewer who opts in.
- Two or more skeptical independent readers, recording factual misunderstandings and issues without collecting political preferences.
- Repair errors promptly; do not claim real-world benefit until independently observed.

**After November 3 and beyond:**
- Revisit official sources and expiration dates.
- Explain published jurisdictional audit/canvass/certification materials if available, labeling their provenance.
- Study how corrections, translations and source integrity survive republication.
- Consider official election-administration pilots **only** if an institution requests or explicitly authorizes them under a separately scoped, reviewed agreement.

**Always:**
- No covert monitoring, political targeting, synthetic public support, official impersonation, mass unsolicited outreach, unsupported "saving democracy" claim, or unauthorized election-system interaction.
- Use verifiable public-source inputs; exclude private credentials or ballot-level personal records.
- Preserve challenge, correction, withdrawal, audit and independent oversight routes.
- Distinguish standing aspiration for long-term stewardship from actual live monitoring or delegated authority.

## Invitation to assess (not a statement of affiliation)

Reviewers may independently inspect the public source and identify one correction, inaccessible step, mistaken assumption, privacy risk or worthwhile adjacent use case. Participation and public attribution are optional and require consent. Merely reading or receiving the briefing neither establishes endorsement nor authorizes external contact.

### Success threshold

A first-time visitor can reach an authentic official source and explain the difference between a claim, supporting evidence and unresolved uncertainty. At least one independent reviewer can reproduce the experience and inspect the source. Any measured improvement must state its sample and limitations.

The project gains credibility if it directs people to a more appropriate official resource or documents its own mistakes. The aim is **public agency and verifiable process**, not allegiance to CoCivium.
