## Lead: election night is not the finish line

Someone trying to understand an election may need more useful support in the days, weeks and years **after** voting than on the day itself.

Provide trustworthy source paths to:
1. Find original **unofficial** returns, when available, without mistaking a media projection for certified results.
2. Understand ballot processing, canvass and reconciliation.
3. Understand what an audit does, what it samples and where state rules differ.
4. Locate the official **certified** result, where released.
5. Distinguish recounts, audits, administrative corrections, court challenges and investigative findings.
6. Learn from documented process issues without disclosing private voter records or inventing wrongdoing.
7. Recheck source currency, accessibility, translation and correction quality in later months and elections.

This remains useful **even if CoCivium gets no credit or traffic**, because the participant can leave directly for the appropriate official source.

### Current reference trail

- [Election Assistance Commission: election results, canvass and certification](https://www.eac.gov/election-officials/election-results-canvass-and-certification), updated 2026-07-30: election-night reports are unofficial, procedures and timing vary by jurisdiction.
- [EAC: Where can I find election results?](https://www.eac.gov/where-do-i-find-election-results), 2025-12-23: state/territorial election offices hold authoritative results information.
- [EAC: Why do results change after election night?](https://www.eac.gov/why-do-election-results-change-after-election-night), 2025-12-23: continued eligible ballot counting, canvass, audits, recount distinctions.
- [NCSL: Post-election audits](https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/post-election-audits), updated 2026-09-24: law, method and timing differ by state; the label "audit" is not one universal procedure.
- [USAGov: Official reporting routes for voting concerns](https://www.usa.gov/voter-fraud): use lawful channels for suspected wrongdoing; do not publish a raw complaint as a finding.

These are source pointers; they are not a claim to have verified a particular 2026 jurisdiction's results.

## CoAll donor integration: many views, not many unverified authorities

The user supplied a concept from another session proposing that almost anything may be represented as a node, relation, state, transition, pattern, absence, boundary, uncertainty, observer, resource or question. A node may be reflected, folded, compressed, retired or rehydrated; the ontology may itself evolve.

Treat this as an **exploratory representation contract**, not a literal claim of infinite operational compute or equivalent physical realities:

- The addressable *candidate* field may be enormous.
- The active computation, publication and receiver frontier must be bounded and measured.
- A possible participant or perspective is not an observed, embodied or consenting participant.
- A view from an AI model is a provisional analysis, not an eyewitness account.
- Ten agents trained on overlapping material are not ten independent primary sources.
- Spiritual, ET, virtual, nonmaterial and mythic perspectives can inspire hypotheses or art; they do not, without independently verifiable evidence, become election facts.
- Unknowns and absence must be representable without silently turning into either fraud accusations or assumed proof of correctness.

### A review packet shared across independently consenting sessions

A proposed review object carries:

\`object_id | version | jurisdiction_scope | process_stage | primary_source_uri | published_at | observed_at | producer_identity | producer_class | permission_scope | source_lineage | epistemic_class | claim | independent_support | credible_counterevidence | alternate_explanations | unknowns | confidentiality | affected_people | proposed_action | stop_conditions | public_projection | readproof | receiver_ack\`

Candidate epistemic values: \`OBSERVED_FROM_SOURCE\`, \`INFERRED\`, \`HYPOTHESIS\`, \`DISPUTED\`, \`UNKNOWN\`, \`RETRACTED\`, \`SUPERSEDED\`.

Candidate perspectives to **invite**, never impersonate or assume active: election administrator, legal reviewer, local access advocate, security professional, statistician, skeptical reader, researcher, journalist, AI evaluator, archiving observer, language translator and humor/creative reviewer. Any actual opinion remains attributed to its **real producer**, with consent where necessary.

**CoPatterna+ rule:** repeated assertions are one source lineage until independently verified. Count *independent underlying records and methods*, not the number of mirrors, posts, sessions or agreeing models.

**CoNull+ rule:** model not-yet-available, redacted, uncollected, disputed and inaccessible evidence as different states.

**CoTurtle+ rule:** preserve immutable prior versions and exactly what changed, so failures and corrections remain inspectable.

**CoAlive+ rule:** label observability separately from liveness. A page or task being discoverable does not mean a real scheduled process is running.

## Lifecycle: don't confuse a repeating loop with progress

\`QUESTION -> PRIMARY_SOURCE_FOUND -> SOURCE_AUTHENTICATED -> CONTEXT_CHECKED -> ALTERNATIVES_IDENTIFIED -> CHALLENGED -> REVIEWED -> PUBLIC_EXPLANATION -> RECEIVER_READBACK -> USEFULNESS_OBSERVED -> RETEST/REFORM\`

A new source or a change in law invalidates old context; preserve both versions.

Record every test outcome with \`PASS | FAIL | INCONCLUSIVE | HELD | NOT_RUN\`, its exact method, source version and limitations. A failure selects among:

- **REPAIR:** a known technical defect and independent acceptance criterion exist.
- **REFRAME:** the hypothesis/test was wrong; replace it, preserving the negative result.
- **RETRY_BOUNDED:** retry only with changed conditions, meaningful wake trigger and a finite budget.
- **REFER:** source authority or expertise belongs with another legitimate organization; link to them.
- **STOP/RETRACT:** privacy risk, inaccurate official election instructions, intimidation risk, unverified named allegations, rights breaches or uncontrolled false positives.
- **DORMANT:** no current independent evidence of relevance; preserve a wake condition without active compute.

**Important:** "keep experimenting until we unfail" is only appropriate when renewed investigation remains safe, lawful and worthwhile. No project can guarantee that every failed experiment will eventually pass. Some failures are signals to discontinue the intervention.

## Value and maintenance

A sustainable project minimizes:
- elapsed time for a reader to find the **correct official source**;
- misunderstandings about unofficial counts, audits, certification and recounts;
- unsupported accusations or public leakage of private data;
- repeated manual handoffs and broken or stale references.

It maximizes:
- correctly cited, dated source explanations;
- accessibility and localization quality;
- independent reviewers' ability to reproduce and challenge a claim;
- documented corrections after counterexamples;
- voluntary contribution and the ability to exit CoCivium entirely.

Use limited, source-specific checks and actual receivers instead of imaginary infinite autocycles. A task configuration is not evidence of a current job run; a public source link is not an actual voter reading it.
