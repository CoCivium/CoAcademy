03 / National, global and future scrutiny

Comparing different countries requires more than converting currencies

Can this calculate the integrity of an election?

No. Campaign spending per ballot does not measure fraud, corruption, administrative reliability or voting rights. A verified legal violation, a source coverage gap, a funding rule and an audit result have different evidentiary meanings.

Can we compare different countries?

International IDEA supplies a public comparative political-finance framework. Country rules, disclosure coverage, currency and purchasing power, reporting periods and election systems differ. Compare the definitions and documented controls first; don't manufacture a global integrity grade from this ratio.

Could indirect regional analysis reveal an individual's vote?

No reliable individual choice follows from a local aggregate. Inferring personal political preferences from group statistics invites ecological fallacy and privacy risks. We intentionally exclude voter-file joins, precinct-level targeting and predictive political profiling.

What makes AI-assisted evolution genuinely faster?

New independent evidence, a confirmed correction, a fresh counterexample, or a demonstrated useful outcome. Repeating a calculation across a million agents does not provide a million independent observations. Preserve failure receipts; repair, reframe, refer, stop or retry with a changed hypothesis and bounded budget.
