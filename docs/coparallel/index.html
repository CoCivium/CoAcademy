04 / Challenge the experience

Inspired by scientific-explanation standards such as Curt's CORE emphasis on depth, insight and impact. These are questions, not claimed performance scores.

RigorAre references and mathematical claims accurate?
InsightDoes a comparison reveal a useful distinction?
ClarityCan a first-time reader explain the disagreement?
FalsifiabilityCan evidence actually weaken a claim?
AgencyDoes the reader control navigation and exit?
OutcomeDid a real reader accomplish something useful?
Unmeasured: independent reader comprehension, scientific correctness review, public reach, accessibility acceptance, external receiver ACK and any autonomous runtime benefit.
