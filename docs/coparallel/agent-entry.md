## The four strongest questions to challenge

1. What does it mean operationally to say that time is not fundamental, while still predicting actual clock measurements?
2. Which parts of relational descriptions are new predictions, and which are mathematically different representations of existing physics?
3. What information is lost when an interview, mathematical paper, or lived experience is projected into a diagram?
4. What measurable educational gain does an interactive, sourced comparison provide over simply reading a primary paper and watching a thoughtful interview?

The original inspiration is Curt's conversation with Julian Barbour: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bprxrGaf0Os.
Barbour's 1994 paper on static configurations and apparent dynamics: https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/12/006.

## Challenge tree, explicitly not new discoveries

- **Relational time:** Specify degrees of freedom, physical clock reconstruction, observable predictions, null examples.
- **Spacetime:** Recover proper time, clock comparison, relativistic observational constraints. Established framework, not an untested theory.
- **Thermodynamic arrow:** Distinguish records and entropy growth from absolute ontology; explain boundary conditions.
- **Quantum-gravity time:** Separate conceptual problems from model-specific mathematical predictions.
- **Phenomenology/meaning:** Ask what lived experience loses when reduced to a computational state, without treating philosophical claims as spectroscopy.

### Separate speculative lane (user-generated conjectures)

**CoNull+ / CoPriMath+ / G_d / CoPlanck** may name research *questions* but are not verified physical entities, equations, measurements, or ET findings.

- **Observer-created quantumness:** An interesting possible model, not a supported account of quantum measurement. Produce a complete formalism and recover Bell-type, interference, and Born-rule results before considering claims of physical novelty.
- **CoNull oscillations:** Define a state space, dynamical/relational update, units and recorded predictions; distinguish visual oscillation from a physical signal.
- **G_d residual:** A philosophical/metaphysical symbol unless an operational, independently discriminating measurement is provided. No claim of divine proof.
- **CoPlanck:** The numerical SI value of h is conventional through unit definitions, but quantum physical relationships are not arbitrary. A change in units does not alter dimensionless observations or quantum phenomena. Source: https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/meet-constants.
- **ET observer standard:** An imaginative hypothesis about conventions used by unknown civilizations, not evidence of ET-defined physical constants.

This is a *research request for criticism*, not a declaration that these ideas are scientifically correct.
