## Why not blast a million addresses?
The critical missing proof is independent human understanding and usefulness, not more published slogans. An opt-in public post or feed is distinct from unsolicited commercial email, and any distribution must comply with the channel and jurisdiction.

Canada's CRTC says commercial electronic messages generally require consent, identifying information and an unsubscribe mechanism: https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm . The U.S. FTC explains commercial email identification and opt-out duties: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business . A noncommercial research invitation can have different legal status; do not camouflage marketing as a survey.

## Bounded pilot, then evidence-led scale
1. Verify current deployed source, actual outside-browser access, RSS parsing, keyboard/mobile use and source correction routes.
2. Invite a few willing first-time readers through an actually authorized, general-interest public channel. Do not infer or scrape private voter groups, journalist inboxes or political preferences.
3. Seek a reproducible finding: a corrected reference, accessible navigation improvement or completed practical task.
4. Log source version, consent scope, delivery receipt where possible, independent source lineage, correction and outcome. Treat opt-out as final.
5. Correct, redesign, stop or expand to a second channel only if useful independent results justify the cost and risk.

## Content object contract
Each adaptation should preserve identity, source version, actual origin and author, format, factual caveats, accessibility, license and rights checks, destination, authorizing actor, publication timestamp, receiver readproof, corrective link and any observed benefit. Another AI's repetition is not an independent fact.

The CoCivium Master Plan assigns the main mission role to cocivium.org, a policy-facing role to copolitic.org and an alias role to copolitic.com. Those are not verified active domains today. The separate CoPolitic-site repository now has an honest GitHub README with neutral source links, but it is not a verified deployed website. The live DNS, certificates, redirects, mirrors and rights to post require distinct provider-side receipts.

CoStead and CoGateway remain candidate storage/relay substrates rather than connected distribution nodes in this wave. X2 is explicitly untouched.

A connected Metricool brand lists a YouTube channel and Bluesky profile, with no scheduled posts returned for the inspected period. This establishes neither CoCivium branding authority nor permission to auto-post. Existing RickPublic/Substack publication plans have no connected Substack publication receiver here. WeTube refers to several unrelated platforms, not a verified user-controlled destination. A short-video storyboard is not a video; a chapter outline is not a published book.

## Public feedback route
CoAcademy GitHub Issues are enabled; account sign-in is required. The provided public issue templates prohibit private voter records, secrets, identifiable allegations or health data. For real voting rights incidents, redirect to https://www.usa.gov/voter-fraud . Publishing a public issue template does not prove anyone has used it or that a maintainer is continuously watching.

## Maintenance and evidence
Source code, successful Pages workflow, outside host readback, real participant completion, useful correction, and recurring subscription must remain separate statuses. RSS here is a static snapshot until an authorized publication workflow updates the exact file and verifies the host. Failures may lead to repair, reframing, retirement, dormant state or bounded retry.

ALL_RELATIONS_ADDRESSABLE does not imply ALL_SUBSTRATES_ACTIVE or ALL_PEOPLE_CONTACTED. Public usefulness and voluntary co-creation are the growth targets, not artificial engagement or spam.

## Branding, reuse rights and failure-domain status (R0)

- [CoRelay public product/brand contract](./product_contract_r0.json) distinguishes CoCivium, CoPlay, Civic Integrity and the still-unbound dedicated CoVIA adapter. Metricool account connection is a read-only channel fact, not proof of CoCivium-branded publishing permission.
- As of the October 24 repository-tree inspection (89 objects), **no license/NOTICE file was found** in CoAcademy. This does not prove that no source text contains a licensing grant; it means an unrestricted FOSS redistribution license was not verified. Original content and third-party marks/assets must be rights-checked before promising forks, remixes, books, or redistribution.
- Source, Pages, feed and public issues share **GitHub as one failure domain**. Multiple URLs do not prove redundancy. An independent remote mirror and a cold restoration into another host are unproven and remain important next experiments.
- CoPolitic.org returned 404 during a recent public fetch; the planned .com alias has no verified redirection, and its source repository is a placeholder. Do not migrate readers to those domains until independent ownership/DNS/HTTPS receipts exist.
- The safest useful growth loop remains: one source → inspectable demonstration → explicit voluntary first use → source-specific correction → reproduced fix → justified distribution. Repeated AI consensus and page impressions do not substitute for that.
