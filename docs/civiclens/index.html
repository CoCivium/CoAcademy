04 / CoLearn+ source-check experiments

Can you distinguish a claim from a verified answer?

Claim: Every U.S. state has identical voter-registration deadlines.
Not supported. Vote.gov says registration deadlines vary by state or territory. Read the official registration guidance.
Claim: Election-night reports always equal certified final results.
Not supported. The EAC distinguishes unofficial election-night totals from canvassing, certification and recount procedures. See the Election Assistance Commission FAQ.
Claim: The federal election calendar tells each voter which polling place to use.
Not supported. Polling locations are administered by state and local officials. Read USAGov polling-place guidance.
