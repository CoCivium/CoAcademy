# CoPlay+ Campaign Product R0: the world's easiest invitation to test a claim

**2026-10-24** | **Status:** `PUBLIC_CREATIVE_PILOT__NO_PAID_ADS__NO_BULK_BROADCAST__IMPACT_UNMEASURED`

## Lead

**You were never supposed to be the operating system.**

This is a public invitation to laugh at the unglamorous burden of coordinating supposedly intelligent software, try one small evidence-backed alternative, and help improve it if you wish.

The goal is not to claim the largest ad campaign in history. The goal is a **replicable, voluntary, useful experiment** with transparent evidence and genuinely low participation friction.

A person should be able to encounter the joke, understand the problem, see exactly what CoCivium has and hasn't built, and leave, contribute, or share without signing up.

## Six-campaign public experiment portfolio

**[Try six separate campaign activities](./campaigns/)** · **[Read the machine campaign registry](./campaigns/campaigns.json)**.

One demo is not the strategy. Six independent activities now illustrate distinct campaign mechanisms: portable handoff packet, source/evidence quiz, fictional spoof generation, live page-presentation toggles, direct U.S. official election-source routes, and inspiration-to-counterexample test notes.

Every lane has its own independently testable outcome, limits, counterexample and opt-in sharing rules. The portfolio remains public experimental source, not real audience impact or an automatic distribution engine.

**Next proof:** validate the whole portfolio in an actual browser and recruit consenting first-use participants without profiling them. Do not confuse a synthetic check with a human outcome. Scaling decisions should promote demonstrated utility and maintainability, not raw theoretical fanout.

## Content design: a reproducible CoPlay object

Every creative candidate should carry:

`id | version | audience-selected context | recognisable problem | fictional hook | satire disclosure | factual assertion(s) | evidence and limitations | suggested voluntary useful action | accessible adaptations | public source URI | release/permission gate | distribution receipt | independent reaction | measured benefit | successor | rollback`

That makes humor portable across Substack, short-form video, social posts, audio, comics, CoFutureSurf, CoOrb, language translations and local community conversations **without letting facts drift differently on different surfaces**.

The original creative object can have many rendered projections, while the receipts, provenance and factual limitations remain attached.

## Distribution as product, not megaphone

1. **Canonical front door:** One genuinely readable landing page, with GitHub source, stable URL, current version and manifest. Handle `cocivium.org`/DNS separately when owner credentials and independent tests are actually available.
2. **Human-shareable units:** Short spoof, clear caption, direct link, visible satire disclaimer, genuinely useful next step. Create text/audio/video/social renderings only when their actual tools and distribution route are authorized.
3. **Machine-discoverable units:** Same facts in HTML, human description, RSS/Atom or JSON feeds, `llms.txt` and typed graph metadata. These are discovery aids, not commands to independent AIs.
4. **Opt-in creator participation:** Editors, comedians, journalists, makers, educators and skeptics can fork/criticize/remix with appropriate rights and source pointers. Respect opt-outs. No unsolicited network-wide 'bombs'.
5. **Value-first recurrence:** People return if a test produced useful improvement; never imply an absent always-on job, receiver or subscription merely because a feed exists.
6. **Failing forward:** Detect broken links, misunderstood satire, inaccessible formats, misleading claims and failed attempts. Correct the source and propagate revisions through authorized adapters with readproof.

The scaling mechanism is **many independent contributions around one reliable shared evidence graph**, not millions of open provider tabs or a single massively repeated ad.

## Public experience and measurable outcomes

Order of proof:

`PUBLIC_SOURCE -> SUCCESSFUL_DEPLOYMENT -> INDEPENDENT_NETWORK_REACHABILITY -> ACCESSIBLE_INTERACTION -> CORRECT_COMPREHENSION -> USEFUL_ACTION -> VOLUNTARY_REPEAT/SHARE -> AUDITABLE_IMPROVEMENT`

An early, **consenting** first-use pilot can ask five diverse volunteers to inspect the page with no briefing. Assess whether they recognize satire, locate the working example, separate proof from hype, and report confusing wording. Five participants may reveal problems but are not representative of the population or evidence of viral growth.

No hidden analytics, session fingerprinting, covert surveys, election persuasion, or political preference inference. Voluntary feedback should be minimal, revocable and not linked to private device identity. Accessibility testing requires actual keyboard/mobile/screen-reader checks.

Use a simple release ledger:

`version | exact Git blob | Pages workflow | outside HTTPS/DOM proof | current satire disclaimer | broken links | accessibility issues | first-use trials | opt-in shares | value demonstrated | changes made`

Do not show a success percentage when there is no denominator. Do not confuse a simulated fan-out with a real recipient acknowledgement.

## The 3-wave pilot, not an endless blast

- **Wave A: one useful opening.** Verify CoPlay in a real browser, its official research links, status, mobile/keyboard access and disclosure.
- **Wave B: three distinctive executions.** Compare three honest creative hooks through voluntary first-use reactions. Choose by comprehension and usefulness, not merely reactions.
- **Wave C: independent derivative participation.** Release reusable copy/format templates with source pointers; accept corrections; use social/newsletter distribution only where an authenticated, specifically appropriate channel exists and returns receipts.

Later waves may expand to CoArt/CoAwe, CoSong, interactive simulations, multi-language localizations, CoOrb projections and externally hosted mirrors after provenance and policy checks.

## Civic/election lane separation

Keep civic-information content factual and visibly separate from brand satire. Provide authoritative registration, voting-method and polling place resources immediately. Do not impersonate election offices, use fabricated candidate/official statements, infer political identity, or optimize for electoral persuasion. Elections are a public-service information test, not a conversion objective.

Reference: [FEC information on state/local election administration](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/election-results-and-voting-information/) and [EAC voter FAQs](https://www.eac.gov/voters/voter-faqs).

## Truth in advertising / rights / community

Make fictitious claims visually clear and independently readable without following a hidden footer link. Never present a parody as a verified news report. Disclose real compensated endorsements and source relationships. Do not invent celebrities, citizen testimonials, adoption numbers, published results, permissions, awards or independent audience feedback.

Reference: [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/truth-advertising/advertisement-endorsements).

Privacy-first, accessible, FOSS-friendly and inclusive participation are design goals. Third-party art/music/likenesses require applicable rights; no automatic assumption that public appearance grants a license.
