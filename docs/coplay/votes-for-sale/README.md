## Source-backed facts, not a voice-of-God campaign

1. [Federal Election Commission, October 1, 2026](https://www.fec.gov/updates/statistical-summary-of-18-month-campaign-activity-of-the-2025-2026-election-cycle/): reported financial **disbursements** cover January 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026:
   - 2026 congressional candidate committees: **$2,036.7 million**
   - Political party committees: **$1,008.1 million**
   - Political action committees: **$6,549.2 million**
   These are distinct reporting categories. Internal money transfers, outlays and disbursements require reconciliation; do **not** add them to claim the final cost of the 2026 elections, or divide by future November turnout to manufacture a current cost of winning a vote.
2. [FEC methodology](https://www.fec.gov/campaign-finance-data/about-campaign-finance-data/methodology/) and [adjusted summary accounting](https://www.fec.gov/data/?cf=downloadable) explain why transfers, receipts, outlays, independent spending and reporting scope must be distinguished.
3. Actual **criminal vote buying** is not merely imaginary: a [DOJ November 30, 2022 release](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/former-louisiana-police-chief-city-councilmember-and-additional-co-conspirator-sentenced-vote) describes sentences after a Louisiana scheme paying or offering payment to voters in 2016 federal-election contests. This adjudicated historic case must not be generalized to current candidates, all voters or any unreviewed locality. Allegations, charges, pleas, convictions, appeals and confirmed findings are distinct stages.
4. [International IDEA Political Finance Database](https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/political-finance-database) documents political-finance *rules*, disclosure, oversight and sanctions. A law's existence does not prove enforcement, and differences across countries make simple score combinations misleading.
5. [Transparency International CPI 2025](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi/2025) reports *perceived public-sector corruption* using multiple source assessments. It does not measure actual vote-buying incidence, validate particular 2026 ballot counts, or certify a local election.
6. [EAC election-administration sources](https://www.eac.gov/election-officials/election-results-canvass-and-certification) document the distinction between unofficial counts, canvass, audits and certification. [USAGov](https://www.usa.gov/voter-fraud) provides official routes for potentially genuine concerns, with privacy and due process.

## A source-evidence card for every public financial claim

Record:
`claim_id | scope | jurisdiction | election_type | election_year | source_publisher | original_url | source_document_date | accessed_at | numerator_type | denominator_type | accounting_scope | transfer_adjustment | value_and_units | alternate_explanations | credible_counterevidence | uncertainty | confidentiality | correction_status | independent_readproof`

**Example interpretations to keep separate:**

| Statement | Evidence class | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committee reported $X in disbursements through June 2026 | Official report, subject to data revisions and definitions | Money used to buy ballots |
| Organization donated or spent $Y | Public filing in a lawful category | A specific policy favor in return |
| Donor claims "excellent ROI" | Self-interested assertion, until independently evaluated | Electoral causality or corruption |
| Court convicted individuals of vote buying | Specific court-reviewed case with documented jurisdiction and date | Prevalence in all elections or a different region |
| CPI country score | Method-dependent perceived public-sector corruption | A direct local election audit |
| Campaign message reached N people | Platform or provider estimate requiring independent validation | Persuasion, vote change or impact |
| Count changed after election night | Unofficial count snapshots needing original sourcing | Fraud by itself |

## The "whale ROI" question without a political targeting manual

An ethical **public-interest ROI explainer** can trace information, not optimize the donor's persuasion strategy:

- **Money trace:** Publicly reported contributions and non-overlapping disbursements, with period, legal category and source.
- **Disclosure trace:** Whether transaction purposes, recipients and reporting dates are publicly available; note legal exceptions.
- **Public action trace:** The existence of policy statements, legislation, procurement or enforcement decisions **as independent observations**, not proof that spending caused them.
- **Evidence gap:** Independent causal attribution, undisclosed influence and allegations of quid pro quo remain `UNKNOWN` unless separately substantiated.
- **Citizen benefit:** Source clarity, public ability to scrutinize claims, corrections and legal accountability without political-personality targeting.

Do not produce tailored persuasion advice for a party, candidate or wealthy donor; do not identify persuadable neighborhoods, infer voters' preferences, or broadcast regional influence analyses to residents because of where they live.

## An integrity dashboard with no invented single verdict

The most defensible public *measurement* separates dimensions:

| Dimension | Possible public evidence | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Political finance disclosure | FEC and jurisdictional filing publication, dates, category definitions | Completeness differs, legality is not morality |
| Institutional oversight | Applicable disclosure laws, independent inquiry processes, documented enforcement | Rules on paper may not be followed |
| Election verification accessibility | Canvass, certification, public audit documentation, accessible official results | Administration varies by jurisdiction |
| Complaints and confirmed findings | Authorized findings with disposition and opportunity for correction | Reports are not convictions; incidence rates require denominators |
| Voter participation and rights | Official aggregate data, accessibility and procedural research | No individual ballot inference |
| Public corruption perceptions | Transparency International CPI and described methods | Country-level perceptions are not election findings |

Do **not** produce a universal locality "fraud likelihood", hidden voter quality score, or a single cross-country moral ranking without a valid common definition, substantive independent methods, published uncertainty, independent review and safeguards against misuse. Existing external indices should always retain their publisher and methods rather than being laundered as CoCivium findings.

## Is 2026 the "most visible and analysed election in history"?

**Not established.** That is a testable hypothesis, not a title to bestow on an election. To evaluate it, specify a time window, comparable historical elections, a definition of "visibility" (independent news/public document readership, campaign spending reporting, audit publication, media coverage, official data access), archival coverage, language biases and whether exposure became *understanding*. Avoid turning raw media impressions or a trillion synthetic analyses into independent public scrutiny.

## Campaign as an ethical self-replicating public tool

A successful campaign derivative can be:
- A clearly marked parody advertisement.
- A five-minute falsifiable claim-check exercise.
- An accessible source card or a verified correction.
- A nonpartisan public talk or classroom illustration.
- A source-linked translation or an independent fact check from a consenting collaborator.
- A publicly sourced court-case chronology, maintained with case status and due process.
- A reader who finds a better official resource and no longer needs CoCivium.

Each derivative includes `version | human-readable disclaimer | public primary source | validity window | relevant alternatives | method | missing information | correction path | voluntary next action | actual publication receipt`.

Make comedy a source of curiosity, never a false government notice. A public outreach release requires separately scoped channel authority, truthful disclosures and real delivery receipts. **No paid placement, unsolicited outreach, investor solicitation or region-specific political message has been authorized or sent through this experiment.**

## Scale, fast cycles and the honest stopping rule

`Useful verified improvement / resource + delay` is a better target than maximum repeated cycles.

Collect **independent** sources and counterexamples; retain failed outputs and improve tests. Progress can mean narrowing scope, fixing, referring, suspending, retracting or permanently stopping a method. Endless rerunning without new evidence is a resource leak, not an evolved proof.

Respect a real receiver's permissions, never infer endorsement from an AI-generated opinion, never interpret an "all perspectives" ontology as evidence of non-human observation, and never claim infinite active resource capacity.
