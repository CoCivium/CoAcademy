Built with CoCivium?

For embedded components, a restrained source-linked attribution could be more useful than a giant umbrella logo. “Built with CoCivium” is proposed language, not a certification. Before showing it, name the actual component and version, its license, test result, responsible operator and any revocation or correction path.

“Intel Inside” is a reference to ingredient branding, not a template to copy or a claim of Intel association.

More source and design boundaries

Shared evolution belongs in versioned patterns, not unexplained automatic rewrites. For translations, preserve complete meaning and review by fluent readers before calling a locale production-ready. For glyph modes, publish decoder, loss ledger and plain-text fallback. Satire and speculative ET styles must never alter factual claims or user permissions.
